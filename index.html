Who we are

More than a
track team.

We coach the whole athlete. Every practice is a chance to get faster, but it's also a chance to learn goal-setting, teamwork and how to show up for yourself and your teammates.

01

Development first

Age-appropriate training that builds proper mechanics, speed and endurance for beginners and experienced runners alike.

02

Character & discipline

Respect, accountability and work ethic are expected at every practice and every meet.

03

Family & community

Parents, coaches and athletes work as one team. When one Falcon rises, we all rise.
